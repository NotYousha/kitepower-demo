How it works

The pumping cycle

A soft wing flies figure-eights crosswind at high tension, reeling tether off a drum that drives a generator. Then it depowers and glides back while a motor reels in. Net output: 50 kW average from 90 percent less material than a tower turbine.

Reel-out, 80 s of every 100 sCrosswind flight at high tension drives the generator at 65 kW.

Net: 50 kW averageProven at 20 to 200 kW by TU Delft, Kitepower B.V. and SkySails Power. Loyd (1980) gives the physics: power scales with wing area, the cube of wind speed and the square of lift-to-drag.

The product

Kitepower K-50

One 20-foot container holds the wing, the ground station and the controls. Three choices are specific to our climate: a heated tether drum and de-icing wing coating, an automated mast launch that needs no crew, and a microgrid controller that leaves the Hydro operator in charge.

Market

NL first, then Canada, then the world’s islands

Beachhead: NL isolated systems

20 systems, 50 GWh a year, about 326 K-50 equivalents. Target 24 units by Year 5, seven percent of isolated diesel generation. First sites: Ramea, Black Tickle, Makkovik.

Canada’s remote communities

147 communities rely on diesel, burning more than 740 million litres a year. The federal Wah-ila-toos hub committed $300 million to diesel reduction through 2027.

Global isolated diesel grids

More than 22.5 GW installed across 220 states and regions, most systems under 1 MW. A containerized plant fits ports, barges and mine sites.

Roadmap

From a 5 kW demonstrator to a fleet on the Labrador coast

Phase 1 · 18 months · this application

Prove it flies here

$312,500 budget, $250,000 requested. K-5 demonstrator, 100 flight hours at an NL coastal site, winter icing test, Transport Canada file, two letters of intent. TRL 2 to 5.

Phase 2 · years 2 to 3

Pilot on a real grid

$1.8M. First K-50 on an NL isolated system, Ramea preferred, with 12 months of monitored operation alongside NL Hydro. Green Transition Fund and federal diesel-reduction funding, $750K seed. TRL 7.

Phase 3 · years 4 to 5

Fleet and export

K-50 and K-100 across Labrador and Island systems, then Nunavut and northern Canada, then island states. 24 units, $4.3M revenue, 20 NL jobs, $4M Series A.
